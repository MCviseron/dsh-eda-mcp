chore(release): v0.7.3

Both sides were exercised on the real machine; five real defects fixed and the
effect-scope knowledge moved into the shipped documents.

Fixes:
- extension: text payloads decode by BOM (the BOM CSV was UTF-16LE and arrived as
  replacement characters) — wire 0.4.1
- extension: the dispatch gate now admits FILE_METHODS too, so every File-returning
  export (Gerber, BOM, netlist, screenshot) stops answering 'API method not allowed'
  — wire 0.4.2
- eda_open_document verifies that a tab actually opened and reports it
- PCB writes refuse a net the board does not have (a track on a phantom net passed
  before and left a DRC netlist error)
- the build no longer ships stale extension zips

Performance:
- eda_place_net_labels runs items with bounded concurrency (938 ms/label against
  2.1 s sequential)

Verification (EDA 3.2.186): schematic side exported 1.05 MB project, parsed netlist
(33 parts), pins with pinName/pinNumber, 11 labels enumerated, batch placement and
cleanup; PCB side 289 layers, 17 nets, 33 components, rich DRC violations (1 netlist
error), design audit, via geometry refusal.

Docs: skill chapter 12 'environment and effect scope' plus the system-prompt lines and
README tables, so a fresh session knows that plugin code needs a DSH restart, the
extension needs the zip re-imported, and only the bridge child can be reconnected.
Patch-only wire versions are now the documented rule.

101 tools, 6 test suites, contract-check green.
